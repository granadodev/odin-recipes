Homemade Ice Cream

https://unsplash.com/es/fotos/helado-de-fresa-en-cucurucho-TLD6iCOlyb0

Description

This classic homemade vanilla ice cream is a rich, velvety treat that delivers pure comfort in every spoonful. Made with a smooth base of heavy cream and milk, it strikes the perfect balance of intense vanilla sweetness and refreshing cold indulgence. Whether enjoyed on its own in a waffle cone or paired with your favorite warm desserts, this simple recipe provides a deeply satisfying taste of authentic creamery goodness right from your kitchen.

Ingredients

  • Heavy cream
  • Whole milk
  • Sugar
  • Vanilla extract
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Mix the base: Whisk the sugar, whole milk, and salt together in a medium bowl until the sugar completely dissolves.
  2. Add the flavor: Stir in the heavy cream and vanilla extract until the mixture is fully combined and smooth.
  3. Chill the mixture: Cover the bowl and refrigerate the liquid base for at least 2 hours until it is thoroughly cold.
  4. Churn the ice cream: Pour the chilled mixture into your ice cream maker and churn according to the manufacturer's instructions for 20 minutes.
  5. Transfer and pack: Spoon the soft ice cream into an airtight container and press a piece of plastic wrap directly onto the surface.
  6. Freeze until firm: Place the container in the back of your freezer for at least 4 hours until the ice cream is scoopable and firm.
  7. Scoop and serve: Let the container sit at room temperature for 5 minutes to soften slightly before scooping into bowls or cones.
