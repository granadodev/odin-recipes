Description

This classic homemade bread is a comforting staple that brings the warm, irresistible aroma of a bakery right into your kitchen. Crafted from simple ingredients, it features a golden, crispy crust that contrasts beautifully with a soft, airy, and chewy interior. Perfect for slicing, toasting, or smearing with butter, this timeless recipe offers the deeply satisfying taste of authentic scratch cooking that elevates any meal or morning routine.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Water
  • Yeast
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Activate the yeast: Mix warm water, sugar, and yeast in a bowl. Let it sit for 5 to 10 minutes until it becomes frothy and bubbly.
  2. Mix the dough: Combine the flour and salt in a large bowl, then pour in the yeast mixture and melted butter. Stir until a sticky dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough: Turn the dough onto a floured surface. Knead vigorously for about 8 to 10 minutes until it becomes smooth, stretchy, and elastic.
  4. First rise: Place the dough in a greased bowl and cover it with a damp cloth. Let it rest in a warm place for 1 hour, or until it doubles in size.
  5. Shape the loaf: Punch down the dough to release the air. Shape it into a loaf and place it inside a greased bread pan.
  6. Second rise: Cover the pan and let the dough rise again for about 30 to 40 minutes, allowing it to puff up above the rim.
  7. Bake and cool: Bake in a preheated oven at 375°F (190°C) for 30 minutes until the top is golden brown. Remove from the oven and let it cool completely before slicing.
