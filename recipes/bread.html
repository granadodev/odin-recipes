Chicken and rice

Description

This classic chicken and rice is a vibrant, one-pot comfort dish that perfectly brings together juicy, seasoned chicken and fluffy rice infused with traditional spices. Sautéed with a savory base of garlic, onions, and bell peppers, the rice absorbs the rich flavors of a tomato-infused broth, accented by cumin and a touch of achiote for its signature golden hue. Mixed with tender peas and carrots, this hearty meal offers a colorful, deeply satisfying taste of authentic home cooking that is perfect for any family gathering.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Rice
  • Broth
  • Aromatics
  • Tomato Base
  • Oil
  • Spices
  • Vegetables
  • Garnish

Steps

  1. Sear the chicken: Heat oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Season the chicken with salt and pepper, then brown all sides until golden. Remove the chicken and set it aside.
  2. Sauté the aromatics: In the same pot, add the chopped onion, bell pepper, and minced garlic. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes until they are soft and fragrant.
  3. Add tomato and spices: Stir in the tomato sauce, cumin, and achiote powder. Mix everything well to create a flavorful base.
  4. Toast the rice: Add the white rice to the pot. Stir constantly for about 1 minute so the grains absorb the oils and flavors.
  5. Simmer: Return the chicken to the pot and pour in the chicken broth. Bring the liquid to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover with a lid, and simmer for 15 minutes.
  6. Sear the chicken: Heat oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Season the chicken with salt and pepper, then brown all sides until golden. Remove the chicken and set it aside.
  7. Add vegetables: Stir in the mixed peas and carrots. Cover the pot again and cook on low heat for another 5 minutes, or until the liquid is fully absorbed and the rice is tender.
